About Me

My story and what I bring to the table

I started with Python backends and moved into AI — connecting LLMs to real products people can reach on any device. I built a Gemini-powered scam-risk checker and an event feedback platform that flags urgent issues live as they arrive.

My background in business administration means I always ask who will use what I'm building and what it costs them to adopt it. That thinking drives why my hackathon projects work over USSD, SMS and voice — no smartphone needed.

I'm currently studying Software & AI Engineering at Turing College and IoT & Embedded Systems at Spiro Academy, deepening the bridge between hardware and AI-powered software.
