About Me

My introduction and what I can do

I started with Python backends and have since moved into AI: connecting LLMs to real products, from a Gemini-powered scam-risk checker to an event feedback dashboard that flags urgent problems as they happen.

My background is in business administration, so I think about who will use what I build and what it costs them to adopt it. That is why my hackathon projects work on any phone, with or without internet.
